## Three business-value pillars
### 1. Regulatory & Compliance Exposure ("Audit Survival")
- System Overload Signal: increasing ratio of open-to-closed deviations; potential quality backlog control weakness and inspection exposure.
- Justification Drift Signal: frequent investigations closed with "Human Error" root cause; examine whether deeper process or engineering factors are missed.

### 2. Operational Resilience & Asset Performance ("OEE/Throughput")
- Premature Degradation Signal: micro-deviations/minor drifts concentrated on one manufacturing line as possible early equipment-wear indicator.
- Post-Maintenance Instability Signal: deviation spike within 48 hours after preventive maintenance or calibration; investigate maintenance execution/calibration.

### 3. Financial & Resource Leakage ("Cost of Quality")
- Scrap / Yield Drag Signal: prolonged material containment/quarantine and potential yield or inventory cost exposure.
- SLA Breach Risk: deviations nearing an applicable closure deadline without an approved extension; possible production/customer delivery impact. A 30-day closure window is not a universal regulatory rule.

## Executive wording upgrades
- Repeat deviations happen -> Failed Localized Remediation: Local quick-fixes are masking systemic process gaps.
- Too many open deviations -> Quality System Throughput Constriction: Investigative backlog creating bottlenecks in batch release cycles.
- People are making mistakes -> Workforce Competency or SOP Mismatch: Training curricula may not match actual processes.
- The change caused errors -> Post-Change Instability Signal: Examine change-control impact assessment and deployment.
- Missing reporting deadlines -> Regulatory Reporting Non-Compliance Risk: Potentially missed jurisdiction-specific health authority reporting windows.

## Predictive signaling concept
Analyze deviation *velocity* and trends, including minor deviation patterns, to prioritize preventive investigation and management resources before major quality failures. These are hypotheses requiring data quality checks, confounder assessment, and prospective performance qualification; no unsupported causal or predictive claim.
