# Classification criteria master — draft

All applicable criteria may independently contribute to a severity determination; no arbitrary primary/supporting hierarchy. Source classifications are retained separately. None is not the same as missing/unclassified. Ordinal 0=None, 1=Minor, 2=Major, 3=Critical; codes are not equal-interval scores. Thresholds vary by industry × module × event type × jurisdiction. Do not conflate FDA NAI/VAI/OAI or EU GMP official inspection categories with PROTECT classifications.

PROTECT-adapted pharmaceutical examples (not official regulatory categories): None = no identified deficiency/adverse impact; Minor = limited departure without significant identified impact; Major = significant GMP departure or credible potential quality/compliance impact; Critical = actual or significant potential patient harm, harmful product, or serious critical control failure. Insufficient evidence => Undetermined, never None.

References for later controlled profile development: 21 CFR 211.192; EU GMP Chapter 1; ICH Q10; FDA Data Integrity guidance. Regulatory applicability and interpretations require profile-specific review.
