# PROTECT–GOV–001 — Commercial Software Lifecycle & Validation Governance
**Version:** 0.1 DRAFT FOR FOUNDER APPROVAL
**Owner:** Fischer-Carballo Consulting LLC
**Product:** PROTECT — human-supervised operational performance, quality and risk decision-support software
**Status:** Governance established in controlled draft; not approved for commercial release.

## Policy
PROTECT maintains product-specific requirements, design, risk, traceability, verification, validation, release, maintenance, supplier-audit and customer handoff evidence. Aster and ABI may assist with coding, review and testing but are **not** the product quality system, independent approval authority or evidence custodian. The company is accountable for intended use, release decisions, records and customer commitments. Evidence may originate in ABI workflows only if traceable, reproducible, reviewed and adopted into PROTECT records.

## Release principles
- Human decision authority retained for classification, CAPA effectiveness, quality/compliance decisions and any escalation.
- No production release or external license before documented founder approval and risk-based release gate.
- No claim of ISO certification, FDA approval, GAMP certification, or customer-specific validation.
- ISO 9001-oriented minimum governance; applicable GxP, electronic records, privacy and security controls assessed by customer intended use and jurisdiction.
- Customer validation is separate from supplier product V&V; responsibilities defined in contract.
- Open Q01/Q02 classification, KPI and prediction criteria remain unapproved; F-06 remains on hold.
- Separate product record set and versioning; retain immutable or tamper-evident evidence appropriate to risk and resources.
