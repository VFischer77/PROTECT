# GOV-001-06 — Customer Validation & Supplier Audit Package
**v0.1 DRAFT — approval pending**

## Standard disclosure tier
Product description, intended use and exclusions, version/build manifest, high-level architecture/data flow, supplier quality overview, requirements coverage summary, V&V summary with traceability, risk/control summary, release notes, known limitations, change management, support and security/privacy statements supported by evidence.

## Controlled audit tier (NDA / need-to-know)
Approved detailed protocols/results, traceability, relevant design and risk records, deviations, review approvals and representative source-control evidence. Access logged, time-bounded and scoped to customer intended use.

## Normally excluded
Proprietary source code, unrelated ABI/Aster records, credentials, secrets, other customer data and security-sensitive internals, except as specifically negotiated under controlled safeguards.

## Responsibility matrix
Supplier: product design, supplier verification/validation, controlled releases, product defects, approved documentation.
Customer: intended-use assessment, configuration, site-specific integration, SOPs/training, user permissions, customer system validation and regulated record controls.
Shared by contract: installation/qualification support, interfaces, data migration, incident notification, service levels and change communications.

## Claims discipline
Supplier validation does not automatically satisfy a customer's GxP/Part 11/Annex 11 obligations. No unsupported certification or regulatory-approval claims.
