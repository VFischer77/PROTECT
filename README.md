# PROTECT — Predictive Risk & Operational Intelligence

Design-stage project for Fischer-Carballo Consulting LLC. No code, deployment, or validated predictive capability is represented by this repository.

**Current decision:** Q01 v0.8 conditionally accepted by owner; Q02 design initiated but not approved.

## Scope
Cross-industry analytical intelligence across Quality/GMP, Operational Excellence, Enterprise Risk Management, and Business Continuity/Resilience. Industry and Service Domain are two independent selections configured **once at workspace initialization** under GC-001 and inherited by all applicable modules; no repeated module-level selection.

## Boundaries
Client retains responsibility for integrity of originating source records, regulated quality decisions, product disposition and CAPA approvals. PROTECT analyzes client-provided records. F-06 is out of Q01 scope/on hold. No code or external action is authorized by these design documents.
