# GOV-001-01 — Software Development & Maintenance Procedure
**v0.1 DRAFT — approval pending**

## Lifecycle
1. Approve intended use, market, user roles, exclusions and customer responsibilities.
2. Baseline product and module requirements with unique IDs, risk and acceptance criteria.
3. Approve architecture, interfaces, data flow, third-party components and AI assistance boundaries.
4. Implement changes on controlled branches; require peer/independent review proportionate to risk.
5. Verify against requirements, record actual source revision, environment, test inputs, outputs and anomalies.
6. Validate representative intended-use workflows; document limits, residual risk and human review.
7. Review release package, approvals and configuration manifest before publishing.
8. Maintain defect triage, customer feedback, monitoring, support, patch and retirement records.

## Roles and separation
Developer/ABI/Aster: proposes or implements and prepares evidence.
Product owner: approves scope, intended use and commercial acceptance.
Quality reviewer: challenges requirements, risks, evidence and unresolved anomalies; may be a designated competent person independent of the specific change when practical.
Release authority: named founder(s), not autonomous agent.
A person may hold multiple roles in a small company, but must document conflict and compensating independent review for material risk.

## Records
Product requirements, architecture/design, code version, dependency inventory, review log, tests, risk register, deviations/defects, approvals, release notes, change log, retirement and customer notices. Never claim independent qualification solely because an agent generated evidence.
