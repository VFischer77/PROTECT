# P05.7 — Founder Conditional Design Acceptance

**Decision:** CONDITIONALLY APPROVED — DESIGN ONLY  
**Decision date:** 2026-11-06  
**Authority:** Founder review (user-confirmed in project conversation)

## Accepted provisional design baseline
- Recommendations, comments and management decision dispositions are supported conceptually.
- Begin with a simple predefined action/decision dropdown, with future refinement and possible customization.
- Support comment/rationale entry and proposed action descriptions.
- Preserve a future `Initiate CAPA` interface capability, disabled pending separate eQMS connector qualification and authorization.
- PROTECT is **not a CAPA repository**; the customer's external eQMS remains authoritative for CAPA creation, execution, effectiveness and closure.

## Open design and implementation items
1. Final dropdown wording, configuration and governance.
2. Authenticated, timestamped, traceable comment history and permissions.
3. Management decision/action-detail validation and disposition rules.
4. Qualified, human-authorized external eQMS interface; success acknowledgement and error handling.
5. UI integration with the agreed compact desktop/mobile module selector.
6. Focused technical qualification and founder usability review.

## Restrictions
Conditional design approval does not constitute technical qualification, validation, cybersecurity approval, commercial release, production deployment, external eQMS writes, or authorization to create a CAPA repository. No previously open classification mapping is finalized by this decision.
