# Q01 v0.8 — Conditionally accepted architecture

**Owner decision:** Conditional design acceptance. This is an approved *architecture baseline*, not approval of all detailed criteria or software implementation.

## Purpose
Assess deviation and investigation performance; identify recurrence, systemic weaknesses and risk indicators; evaluate cross-module relationships; support eventual validated prediction of deviation frequency deterioration, future significant events, recurrence, and systemic deterioration.

## Lifecycle
Event identification; classification; containment; product/process/batch impact and investigation scope expansion; investigation evidence and root cause; linked quality disposition/escalation; CAPA linkage; closure; effectiveness verification; recurrence monitoring.

## Investigation indicators IP-01–IP-13
IP-01 Timeliness; IP-02 Completeness; IP-03 Root Cause Quality; IP-04 Impact Assessment Quality; IP-05 Investigation Depth; IP-06 CAPA Linkage; IP-07 CAPA Appropriateness; IP-08 Recurrence; IP-09 Investigation Reopening; IP-10 Investigation Trend; IP-11 Cross-Module Evidence; IP-12 Management Oversight; IP-13 CAPA Effectiveness Verification Result.

IP-13: Effective / Not Effective after verification; Pending until verified. Related recurrence Yes/No, with chronology and failure-mechanism evaluation. Q02 owns authoritative CAPA effectiveness result. Q01 flags potential reassessment without overwriting client records. Distinguish investigation quality from execution timeliness; no arbitrary combined score.

## Cross-module analysis
Q01-ANA-008 integrated cross-module analysis; 009 temporal relationships; 010 systemic patterns; 011 distinguish cause/association/hypothesis; 012 intervention effectiveness; 013 incremental predictive value; 014 evidence traceability.

Inputs: Q02 CAPA, Q03 Change Control, Q04 Internal Audits, Q05 Supplier/External Audits, G01 authority intelligence, Q06 Training, Q07 Validation, Q08 Lab/OOS/OOT, Q09 Complaints, Q10 Product Quality, Q11 Post-Market, O01 Maintenance, O02 Process, O04 CI, O06 Management OFIs, relevant R/B/S modules. Cross-module inputs retained; no narrowing to audits alone.

## Configuration
Q01 inherits the two selections made once during PROTECT workspace initialization under GC-001; Q01 has no separate Industry or Service Domain selector. Industry-specific classification criteria; all applicable CC-01–CC-30 evaluated on their merits. Preserve source classifications, avoid automatic overwrite, distinguish official regulatory categories. Pharmaceutical profile first, followed by biotech and medical device; broader industries as appropriate.

## Scope boundary
Client owns source-record integrity, underlying eQMS, release/disposition decisions, CAPA approvals. F-06 historical record integrity and source governance out of Q01 scope and on hold. PROTECT may identify unavailable analytical inputs, but does not take over source-record integrity.
