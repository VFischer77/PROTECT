# ABI-REC-001 — Shared contextual recurrence analysis (design)

Applicable at minimum to Q01 Deviations, Q02 CAPA, Q04 Internal Audits, Q05 Supplier/External Audits; extend to Q03 Changes, Q06 Training, Q08 Lab, Q09 Complaints, O01 Maintenance and other relevant modules.

1. Keywords, synonyms, semantic similarity identify *candidates* only.
2. Context: failure mechanism, root cause, process, product, equipment, site, conditions, intervention intent, chronology, and counterevidence.
3. Different words may reflect the same mechanism; identical words may describe unrelated failures.
4. Judgment: confirmed, probable, possible, no recurrence identified; provide linked record references and rationale; human review for consequential decisions.
5. Differentiate recurrence, common systemic cause and mere correlation.
6. CAPA result remains client-approved Effective/Not Effective, Pending until verified. Flag suspected failure; never automatically overwrite.

ABI-SYS-001 emerging systemic risk recognition *before recurrence* is reserved for later predictive integration, not part of immediate Q01 design scope.
