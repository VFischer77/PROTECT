# PROTECT Q03 v0.1 — Consolidated Change Control Design
Status: PROPOSED, owner acceptance pending. Design only. No tests executed.

## Q03.1 Scope
Analyze initiation, impact and risk assessment, approval readiness, implementation, post-implementation verification, and trends. Client retains authority for approvals, source-record integrity, change execution and product decisions. No eQMS functionality.

## Q03.2 Logical inputs
Change ID/type/site/process; trigger/rationale/intended benefits; affected products, processes, equipment, suppliers and systems; impact assessment; risk controls/residual risk; applicable regulatory/filing assessment; approval status; implementation plan/actual dates/deviations; acceptance criteria, verification outcome, unintended effects; linked Q01/Q02 and other records. Required/conditional/optional designation is use-case dependent; missing evidence cannot be scored favorable. Inherit one-time GC-001 workspace Industry and Service Domain, no module-level selectors. Preserve source classification; apply relevant CC-01–CC-30.

## Q03.3 Proposed independent indicators
CH-01 assessment completeness; CH-02 impact adequacy; CH-03 risk adequacy; CH-04 approval readiness; CH-05 implementation timeliness; CH-06 implementation conformity; CH-07 verification completeness; CH-08 post-implementation effectiveness; CH-09 change-related deviations/failures; CH-10 unintended consequences; CH-11 cross-functional coordination; CH-12 change-driven improvement effectiveness. Do not equate on-time implementation with technical adequacy/effectiveness. No unvalidated composite score.

## Q03.4 Contextual intelligence
Link Q01/Q02, Q06/Q07, Q08/Q10, O01/O02, R01–R03 and relevant B/S/G and other modules. Distinguish pre-existing versus change-induced risk, intended versus unintended consequences, temporal association versus supported causality, implementation versus effectiveness. Reuse shared ABI-REC-001 for relevant contextual relationships. ABI-SYS-001 predictive systemic warning remains deferred.

## Q03.5 Proposed acceptance cases — NOT EXECUTED
CHG-01 on-time but inadequate impact assessment; CHG-02 approved but incomplete implementation; CHG-03 change-related deviation with supporting evidence; CHG-04 deviation predating change; CHG-05 intended benefit plus unintended risk; CHG-06 no effectiveness verification; CHG-07 cumulative interacting changes; CHG-08 omitted applicable regulatory impact; CHG-09 shared CAPA/change relationships without double counting; CHG-10 insufficient evidence; CHG-11 GC-001 inherited with no repeated selection; CHG-12 time-respecting predictive baseline qualification.
